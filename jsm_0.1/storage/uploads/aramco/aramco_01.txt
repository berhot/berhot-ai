Saudi Aramco is a Saudi Arabian energy and petroleum company based in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia.

It is one of the largest oil and gas companies in the world. Its main activities include exploring for oil and natural gas, producing them, refining petroleum, producing chemicals, and distributing energy products.

Saudi Aramco was founded in 1933 and later became fully owned by the Saudi government. In 2019, part of the company was listed on the Saudi Stock Exchange, Tadawul.

The company operates major oil fields, refineries, pipelines, and industrial facilities in Saudi Arabia and also has investments and operations in many countries.

Saudi Aramco plays an important role in Saudi Arabia’s economy and energy industry.
