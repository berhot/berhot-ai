Once upon a time there was a small village near a quiet river
The people of the village woke up early every morning
They worked in the fields and came home before the sun went down
In the evening they sat together and told old stories

Once upon a time there was a young girl who loved to read books
She read under a big tree behind her house
Every book showed her a new place she had never seen
One day she decided to write a story of her own

Once upon a time there was an old fisherman who lived by the sea
He had a small boat and a very long net
Every day he went out on the water before the sun came up
He always came home with enough fish for his family

Once upon a time there was a little bird that could not fly
The other birds laughed and flew away without it
The little bird tried again every single morning
One day it opened its wings and flew over the trees

Once upon a time there was a king who wanted to be wise
He asked every traveller to teach him one new thing
After many years he knew a little about many things
The people said he was the best king they ever had
