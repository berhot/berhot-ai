Once upon a time there was a farmer who had a very old horse
The horse was slow but it never stopped working
Every morning they walked together to the field
The farmer said a slow friend is better than a fast stranger

Once upon a time there was a boy who was afraid of the dark
His mother gave him a small lamp to keep by his bed
Each night he turned the light a little lower
One night he slept without the lamp and was not afraid

Once upon a time there was a baker who made bread for the whole town
She woke up long before everyone else
The smell of warm bread filled every street in the morning
People said the day could not begin without her bread

Once upon a time there was a small boat that wanted to cross the sea
The big ships said the sea was too wide for it
The small boat left anyway on a calm morning
After many days it reached the other side

Once upon a time there was a teacher who lived in the mountains
Children walked a long way to sit in her class
She taught them to read and to count and to ask questions
Many years later they came back to thank her

Once upon a time there was a cat that followed a merchant to the market
It sat beside him while he sold his cloth
The people stopped to look at the cat and then bought the cloth
The merchant shared his dinner with the cat every night
